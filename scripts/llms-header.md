# Enva

> Enva is an iPhone and Apple Watch app that brings training, food, sleep, recovery and blood work into one Health Score and one Biological Age, with an AI assistant, Enva Intelligence, that answers from all of it. Built by Olympus Hills Ventures LLC. Coming soon; the waitlist is open at https://getenva.ai/#waitlist.

Key facts:

- Platforms: iPhone and Apple Watch. There is no Android version.
- Connects to Apple Health, Apple Watch, Oura Ring, Peloton and Orangetheory at launch. Garmin, WHOOP and Fitbit are coming soon.
- Scores are computed on the device and compared with the user's own baseline, not a population chart. Each score shows how confident it is.
- Enva Intelligence (the AI assistant) is off until the user turns it on. It receives computed summaries, not raw sensor data, and user data is never used to train models.
- Pricing: 7-day free trial, then $12.99 a month or $89.99 a year (about $7.50 a month). Every feature on both plans. Subscriptions are managed through Apple.
- Enva is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice.
- Contact: hello@getenva.ai (general), support@getenva.ai (support), privacy@getenva.ai (privacy requests).
