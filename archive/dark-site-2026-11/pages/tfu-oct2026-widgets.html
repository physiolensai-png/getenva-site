Enva widgets

Put your scores and progress on your Home Screen or Lock Screen and see them without opening the app. Here is every widget, what it shows, and how to add it.

How to add one See all widgets

14 widgets in this build, plus Lock Screen versions

How to add

Four taps to a widget

  1. Press and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the icons wiggle. For a Lock Screen widget, press and hold the Lock Screen instead and tap Customize.
  2. Tap the plus in the top corner, then search for Enva.
  3. Pick a widget, swipe to the size you want, and tap Add Widget.
  4. Press and hold the widget, then tap Edit Widget to change what it shows. Only the ones tagged Configurable can be changed.

Widgets show the data Enva last synced. If one looks empty, open Enva once and give it a moment.

All widgets

Every widget in one place

Previews use sample data. A good starter set: Health Score for the headline, Recovery Actions for the day, and Hydration so you can log water from the Home Screen.

Health Score

Your daily Enva Health Score at a glance.

Metric

Any single number you choose: a vital, steps, net calories, protein, carbs or fat. Press and hold, then Edit Widget, to change it.

HRV

Heart rate variability against your own range, with the last two weeks behind it.

Resting HR

Resting heart rate against your own range, with the last two weeks behind it.

Body Metrics

Your vitals side by side, each against your own range. You choose which ones.

Health Summary

HRV, resting heart rate, breathing, oxygen, wrist temperature and sleep, each against its ideal range. Tap to open the Health Monitor.

Nutrition

Net calories, protein, carbs and fat as dials. Protein fills toward its goal. Calories, carbs and fat show when they pass their targets.

Net Energy

Calories eaten minus today's workouts, against your target. The wide one adds your planned burn.

Protein

Today's protein, filling toward your goal.

Today

Steps, net calories and protein, progress you can still add to. Mix in any metric you like.

Hydration

Today's water against your target. Tap a button to log a glass without opening the app.

Recovery Actions

Today's recovery checklist. Tap a box to tick off the ones that are up to you, like caffeine cut-off, daylight, sleep and water. Items with a ring check themselves.

Recovery, Sleep and Output

Recovery, sleep and output in one table, with the output to aim for today. The large one adds your workouts.

Reminder

A friendly nudge you can check off, like drink water, get outside or stretch.

Metric (Lock Screen)

Any single number you choose, as a ring or a bar. Press and hold, then Edit Widget, to change it.

HRV (Lock Screen)

Heart rate variability against your own range, as a number or with the label.

Resting HR (Lock Screen)

Resting heart rate against your own range, as a number or with the label.

Protein (Lock Screen)

Today's protein, filling toward your goal.
