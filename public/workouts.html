Three ways in

Start where you are.

Use a ready-made session, build your own, or let Enva write one for you. They all end up in the same place, ready to run.

1 · Pick one

Start from a template.

A library of curated sessions, from strength blocks to HIIT. Open one, change what you like, and go.

2 · Build it

Make your own.

Choose your movements, sets and reps from a catalogue of exercises grouped by the equipment they need. Save it to My Templates.

3 · Ask Enva

Have it written for you.

Give Enva three things: your equipment, your goal and your time. It writes the session, rounds and reps included.

On your wrist

Do it without picking up your phone.

Every workout you build runs on Apple Watch. It shows the movement, the weight and the target for each set, so you can do it and record it from your wrist.

  • One tap to finish each set
  • Heart rate and calories in view while you work
  • A summary when you finish, saved to Apple Health
  • Tap any lift afterward to correct the sets
Strength

See your strength move.

Log each set with its weight and reps, then watch the trend for that movement build over time. You see whether the numbers are going up, not a pile of notes.

  • Weight and reps for every set
  • A progression view for each movement
  • Orangetheory, Peloton and Apple Watch sessions in the same history, with no double counting

Where it connects

Training is the input.

Your training load is one of the three pillars of the Health Score, and it decides how tomorrow's recovery reads. See how your scores work, or ask Enva what to train today.

Suggestions, not prescriptions.

Workouts that Enva writes have not been tested by a coach. Choose weights you can control, stop if something hurts, and talk to a clinician before starting hard training if you have a health concern.
