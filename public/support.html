A weighted estimate of how your body is functioning, independent of your chronological age. Enva builds it from five domains: Cardiovascular Fitness (30%), Recovery Capacity (20%), Activity Profile (20%), Body Composition (15%), and Biomarker Health (15%). A domain you haven't connected yet is left out and the rest are reweighted to compensate, so you're never penalized for data you don't have yet. See Biological Age for the full methodology.
