Biological Age is a weighted estimate of how old your body is functioning — independent of your chronological age. Enva calculates it across five domains: Cardiovascular Fitness (VO₂ Max, HRV, resting HR), Recovery Capacity (sleep quality and HR recovery), Activity Profile (training zones, consistency, variety), Body Composition (body fat %, lean mass), and Biomarker Health (labs like ApoB, HbA1c, CRP). Missing domains are excluded from the calculation — you're never penalized for data you don't have yet.
Apple Health flattens workout data. It knows you did a "cycling" workout — but it doesn't know your Peloton output, resistance, cadence, or leaderboard ranking. It doesn't capture Orangetheory splat points, treadmill pace, or rower 500m averages. These are the metrics that actually tell you whether you're improving. Direct connections give Enva access to the enriched data each platform captures natively.
