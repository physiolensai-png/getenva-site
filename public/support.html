Common questions

Everything, up front.

How much does Enva cost?
$12.99 a month, or $89.99 a year, which works out to about $7.50 a month, about 42% less than paying monthly. Both plans include every feature. Every new subscriber gets a 7-day free trial on whichever plan they pick, so you can try the app before paying anything. One trial per customer.
When will Enva be available?
Enva is coming soon to iPhone and Apple Watch. Join the waitlist to be notified the moment we launch.
Which devices and apps does Enva support?
At launch, Enva connects with Apple Watch, Oura Ring, Peloton, and Orangetheory. Garmin, Whoop, and Fitbit are coming soon. We build direct connections to each ecosystem so you get the full, unflattened data, not just what Apple Health captures.
Do I need an Apple Watch?
No. Enva works with an Apple Watch, an Oura Ring, or neither. It reads your data from Apple Health and your connected services, and you can log workouts, food and sleep by hand. A score that depends on data you have not connected shows lower confidence instead of guessing. Starting a workout from your wrist does need an Apple Watch.
Can Enva build my workouts for me?
Yes. Start from a curated template, build your own from scratch, or hand Enva Intelligence the equipment you have, the time you have and the goal you're chasing, and it writes the session for you, rounds and reps included. Every workout you build also runs on Apple Watch, so you can do it and record it without going back to your phone.
Does Enva work for event training, like HYROX?
Yes. Set a target time and a date for a HYROX® race, a DEKA event, a Dri-Tri, an Orangetheory benchmark, or any goal with a finish line, and Enva reorganizes your training around it: a projection, the single biggest limiter, a plan by phase, and sessions built for exactly what you're missing. HYROX® is a registered trademark of HYROX GmbH. Enva is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by HYROX.
How do I get a recipe into my food log?
Find a recipe in Enva, or ask Enva Intelligence to write one. Enva works out the nutrition for the whole recipe, and you log your portion rather than the whole pot. It goes into your food log and counts toward your daily calories and macros. See Meals for the full walkthrough.
How does Enva work out calories and macros for food it cannot find?
Search the food catalog first, which includes restaurant menu items and alcohol, with the calories from alcohol counted. If you still cannot find it, tap Estimate with Enva Intelligence and describe the food. You get an estimate of calories and macros to log, and you can edit any entry afterwards. For supplements, scan the barcode or the label.
How do I add blood work?
There are three ways: import your results from Apple Health Records if your provider shares records with Apple Health, upload a lab PDF, or enter the values by hand. Not every provider supports Apple Health Records, so PDF upload and manual entry always work. Enva Intelligence only brings your labs into an answer when you ask about them.
What can I ask Enva Intelligence, and how many questions do I get?
Ask about your training, food, sleep, recovery and labs, have it build a workout from the equipment you have, find a recipe, or explain what a score means. You can ask up to 20 questions a day. It answers from your own data, using the part that fits your question, and blood work is only included when you ask about it. It is not medical advice.
Is my health data safe?
Yes. Most of Enva's processing happens on your device: your Health Score, Biological Age, and recovery trends are all computed on your phone. When deeper AI coaching has to run off-device, it uses an enterprise-secure model that cannot use your data for training. We never sell your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
Can I back up or delete my data?
Yes to both, from Settings. Backup and Restore saves a backup file of everything you entered in Enva (manual workouts, strength, nutrition, supplements, blood work), and you can restore it later. Data from Apple Health and connected services is not in the file; it re-syncs when you reconnect. Delete All Data permanently removes every session, connected account, and setting from your device. It cannot be undone. It does not remove anything from Apple Health, including nutrition entries and water that Enva wrote there with your permission. To delete those, open the Health app and go to Sharing, then Apps, then Enva. See About for what this looks like. For any other privacy request, email privacy@getenva.ai.
What is the Enva Health Score and how is it calculated?
A single score from 0 to 100 for how your week is going, read from three pillars, each scored against your own baseline: Readiness, Load quality and Trajectory. Readiness is how recovered you are, Load quality is whether this week's training fits what your body is used to, and Trajectory is the 6-week direction of VO₂ Max, HRV and training load. Scores run Needs Work (0 to 39), Fair (40 to 59), Good (60 to 77) and Excellent (78 to 100). A lower score after a hard week is not a warning. See Your data for the breakdown.
What is Biological Age and how is it calculated?
A weighted estimate of how your body is functioning, independent of your chronological age. Enva builds it from five domains: Cardiovascular Fitness, Recovery Capacity, Activity Profile, Body Composition, and Biomarker Health. A domain you haven't connected yet is left out and the rest are reweighted to compensate, so you're never penalized for data you don't have yet.
Why does Enva connect directly to Peloton and Orangetheory instead of using Apple Health?
Apple Health flattens workout data. It knows you did a "cycling" workout, but not your Peloton output, resistance, cadence, or leaderboard rank. It doesn't capture Orangetheory splat points, treadmill pace, or rower 500m averages, the metrics that actually tell you whether you're improving. Direct connections give Enva the enriched data each platform captures natively.
How do I cancel, and what happens after the free trial?
Subscriptions are managed through Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Enva, and tap Cancel Subscription. If you cancel before the 7-day trial ends, you are not charged. Otherwise the plan you picked starts when the trial ends.
Is Enva a substitute for medical advice?
No. Enva is a functional model built from your physiology, training, and blood chemistry, not a diagnostic tool or a DNA test. A biomarker outside its reference range is a reason to talk to a clinician, not a conclusion. Nothing in the app should be treated as medical advice.

No. Enva is built for iPhone and Apple Watch. Join the waitlist and we will email you when it launches on iPhone.
