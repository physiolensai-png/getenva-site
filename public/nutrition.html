Your baseline

A calorie target built from your metabolism.

Setup takes a minute. Enva computes your resting burn from the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, scales it by how much you have actually trained over the last four weeks, then turns that into daily calorie and macro targets for the goal you pick.

Train more and your energy need rises with it, automatically. You can still override the activity level or the macros by hand at any point.

What Enva tracks

Six things, not just a calorie count.

Calories in versus out is the start, not the whole story. Two days at the same total can leave you recovering very differently.

So Enva scores the composition and the timing too, and every target is set from your own BMR and TDEE, adjusted for the day's actual training load.

Energy balance

Intake against your BMR and TDEE, adjusted for the day's actual training load rather than a fixed activity multiplier.

Macronutrients

Protein, carbohydrate and fat against targets set for your goal: fat loss, muscle gain, or an event build. Protein is tracked per kilogram of body weight.

Micronutrients

Iron, magnesium, vitamin D, B12 and the rest of the panel that shows up in labs and recovery long before you feel it.

Hydration

Water and electrolyte intake, with caffeine tracked read-only from HealthKit so it can inform your recovery cutoff.

Timing & consistency

When you eat relative to training and sleep, and how steady your intake is week to week.

Alcohol

Logged as ethanol grams and standard drinks, so its effect on sleep and recovery is measured rather than guessed.

Getting it in

Log it however it comes.

Your day, itemized by meal with macros on every entry. For the food without a label, Enva estimates it from a photo, a pasted description, or a restaurant's own figures, then shows you the numbers to check before saving.

Barcode scan, bundled restaurant and alcohol catalogs, saved recipes and copy-a-day all feed the same log, so the number that reaches your scores is the one you actually ate.
