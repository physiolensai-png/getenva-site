What Enva tracks

Six things, not just a calorie count.

Calories in versus out is the start, not the whole story. Enva scores the composition and timing of what you eat, because two days at the same calorie total can produce very different recovery.

Energy balance

Intake against your BMR and TDEE, adjusted for the day's actual training load rather than a fixed activity multiplier.

Macronutrients

Protein, carbohydrate, and fat against targets set for your goal: fat loss, muscle gain, or an event build. Protein is tracked per kilogram of body weight.

Micronutrients

Iron, magnesium, vitamin D, B12, and the rest of the panel that shows up in labs and recovery long before you feel it.

Hydration

Water and electrolyte intake, with caffeine tracked read-only from HealthKit so it can inform your recovery cutoff.

Timing & consistency

When you eat relative to training and sleep, and how steady your intake is week to week.

Alcohol

Logged as ethanol grams and standard drinks, so its effect on sleep and recovery is measured rather than guessed.

Where it connects

An input that moves the outputs.

Health Score

Under-fueling a hard week shows up as suppressed recovery inside the Readiness pillar before you notice it yourself.

Sleep & Recovery

Late meals, alcohol, and low carbohydrate availability all register in the next morning's Recovery score.

Biological Age

Protein intake and body composition feed the Body Composition domain; metabolic markers feed Biomarker Health.
