Let's be straightforward with each other.

Most privacy policies are written to give companies the legal cover to do whatever they want with your data. Ours is written to explain exactly how little of your data we actually touch.

At Enva, we believe your health and fitness data belongs to you. This Privacy Policy explains how Olympus Hills Ventures LLC ("Company," "we," "our," or "us") handles information when you use getenva.ai (the "Site") and the Enva mobile application (the "Mobile App") (collectively, the "Services").

By using Enva, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service. If you don't agree, please do not use the Services.

1. The Golden Rule: Your Health Data Stays on Your Phone

We designed Enva so that we don't have to secure your sensitive health data on our servers — because we don't collect it in the first place.

  • Local storage. When you connect Enva to Apple Health or other third-party integrations, that health and fitness data (like your heart rate, sleep metrics, and workout history) is imported and processed locally on your device.
  • No cloud servers. We do not transmit, upload, or store your raw health or biometric data on our company servers.
  • Not subject to HIPAA. Because we are a wellness app and not a healthcare provider, and because we don't store your health data, we are not a "covered entity" under HIPAA.

2. Information We Actually Collect (And Why)

While your health data stays on your phone, we do collect a limited amount of information to keep the app running, offer customer support, and figure out how to improve the product.

A. Information you provide directly

  • Account and contact info: If you create an account or email us for support, we collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • Payment information: We do not collect or see your credit card information. Subscriptions are handled entirely through the Apple App Store, subject to Apple's privacy policy.

B. Information collected automatically (app analytics)

  • Usage data: We collect anonymized, non-personally identifiable data about how you use the app (for example, which screens you visit, what features you click, and crash reports).
  • Device data: Basic information like your device model, iOS version, general geographic region (not precise location), and IP address.
  • Tracking technologies: We use standard analytics tools (like Mixpanel, Google Analytics, or Apple App Analytics) to understand app performance. This data does not contain health information.

3. How Enva's AI Features Work

If you choose to enable Enva's AI-powered wellness insights, here is exactly what happens to your data:

  • Anonymization. When you ask a question or request an insight, Enva compiles the necessary inputs and strips them of personally identifiable information.
  • Transmission. This anonymized payload is sent to Google Cloud's Vertex AI, powered by Gemini.
  • No retention or training. Google processes the data to generate your insight and immediately discards the prompt. As per our enterprise agreement with Google Cloud, your data is never retained, and it is never used to train Google's AI models.
  • No chat history on our servers. Unlike other apps, we do not store your AI conversation history on our servers for QA or debugging. If you delete the app, your AI history is gone.

A note on reproductive health. Because all health data stays on your device, we do not collect, process, or store reproductive health data on our servers, nor is it transmitted to our AI partners unless you explicitly write it into a custom prompt.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Ever.

We only share the limited data we collect (like your email or anonymized analytics) in the following scenarios:

  • Service providers: With trusted companies that help us run our business, like email providers for customer support or crash-reporting software.
  • Legal compliance: If we are legally required to do so by a valid court order, subpoena, or law enforcement request.
  • Business transfers: If Olympus Hills Ventures LLC is acquired, merged, or sold, your contact and analytics data (but not your on-device health data) may be transferred as a business asset.

5. Security and Data Retention

We use commercially reasonable security measures to protect the limited data we do hold, like support emails and account credentials.

  • Account data: Retained as long as your account is active.
  • Analytics data: Retained indefinitely in an aggregated, anonymized format.
  • Health data: Because health data lives on your device, you control the retention. When you delete the Enva app, your local Enva data is permanently deleted from your phone.

6. Children's Privacy

Enva is not meant for anyone under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are between 13 and 17, your use of the app is governed by Apple App Store parental controls. If we learn we have collected data from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

7. U.S. State-Specific Privacy Rights (Including Utah and California)

If you live in a state with comprehensive privacy laws (like California, Utah, Colorado, Virginia, or Texas), you have specific rights regarding your personal data.

Because we do not store your health data, these rights primarily apply to your email address and analytics profile:

  • Right to know or access: You can ask us what personal data we have about you.
  • Right to delete: You can ask us to delete your personal data from our systems.
  • Right to correct: You can ask us to fix inaccurate data.
  • Right to opt out: You can direct us not to "sell" or "share" your data for targeted advertising. We don't do this anyway.

To exercise these rights, email us at hello@getenva.ai. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

"Do Not Track" and Global Privacy Control. We do not track users across third-party websites, so we do not respond to DNT signals. However, we honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals for our website analytics.

8. International Users (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are accessing Enva from the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, Olympus Hills Ventures LLC is the "data controller" for your contact and analytics data.

  • Legal basis: We process this data to fulfill our contract with you (providing the app) and for our legitimate interests (improving the app).
  • Your rights: You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object to processing, and request data portability.
  • Data transfers: Your data is processed in the United States. By using Enva, you consent to this transfer.

To exercise your GDPR rights, contact us at hello@getenva.ai. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Third-Party Links

Enva may contain links to other websites or services, like Apple Health or research articles. We don't control those third parties, and this Privacy Policy does not apply to them. Please read their privacy policies before sharing your data.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will let you know by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and we may also notify you within the app. Your continued use of Enva after the changes take effect means you agree to the new policy.
