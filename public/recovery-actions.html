Recovery Actions · Input

You can't control recovery.
You can control today.

Your Recovery score is a readout, not a dial you can turn directly. Recovery Actions is the input side of the same idea: a short daily list of things that actually move it, surfaced by Enva and tracked automatically where your data allows.

What's on the list

A short list, pulled from your own patterns.

Recovery Actions are not a generic wellness checklist. Each one is drawn from a signal Enva already reads, and most are checked off automatically once the data confirms them, so the list reflects what actually happened, not what you remembered to log.

Caffeine cutoff

Stop caffeine a set window before bed. Tracked automatically from HealthKit; a notification reminds you as the cutoff approaches.

Hydration target

Reach your day's water target, adjusted for training load and heat. Logged as you log intake through the day.

Wind-down window

Start easing off screens and stimulation on a consistent schedule, so sleep onset has a chance to work with you instead of against you.

Consistent sleep and wake time

Hold your bed and wake times within a tight band. Confirmed automatically once the night is logged.

Post-workout protein window

Log protein within the window after a session that calls for it. Shown only on days it actually applies.

Where it connects

A bonus for showing up. Never a penalty for missing a day.

How completion is scored

Completing a Recovery Action adds a small bonus to your Enva Health Score, on top of your actual Recovery score. This rewards the behavior that produces good recovery, not just the outcome.

Skipping an action never subtracts anything. Your Health Score is never worse for a day you didn't check a box. The list is there to help, not to be one more thing you can fail at.

Health Score

Completed actions add a small bonus alongside the Readiness pillar. The underlying Recovery score is unaffected either way.

Sleep & Recovery

Most actions are aimed directly at what drives tomorrow's Recovery score: caffeine timing, wind-down consistency, and sleep schedule regularity.

Nutrition

Hydration and protein-timing actions are logged through the same entries that power your Nutrition tracking.
