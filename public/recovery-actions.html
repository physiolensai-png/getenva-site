Today's plan

A read on the day, then a short list.

Every morning Enva reads your HRV, sleep stages, resting heart rate and yesterday's load, and turns them into one plain sentence: how hard to train today, and why.

Under it sits a ranked plan. Some items Enva calculates and checks off for you. The rest are a handful of habits you tick as you go.

Auto-calculated

Sleep target

Tonight's hours and a target bedtime, set from your typical wake time. Confirmed automatically once the night is logged.

Today's effort

A recommended session type for your recovery level, from an easy zone 2 flush to a normal training day.

Intensity cap

On a low-recovery day, a ceiling on how hard to go, so a single session doesn't stack load on top of what you're still absorbing.

Log it yourself

Morning sunlight

20-plus minutes of daylight early anchors your circadian rhythm and shows up in tonight's sleep depth.

Caffeine cutoff

No caffeine after your set time. A reminder fires as the cutoff approaches; caffeine intake is read from HealthKit.

Hydration target

A water goal for the day, scaled to your body weight and today's training load. Logged as you log intake.

Wind-down & consistent wake time

Ease off screens on schedule, and hold your wake time in a tight band. Both feed directly into tomorrow's Recovery score.

Recovery, through the day

Recovery is a morning number. Battery is a live one.

Your Recovery score sets the charge you wake up with. Battery tracks it draining as you train and move, so by mid-afternoon you can see what you actually have left, not what you had at 7am.

Some apps keep this on its own screen. Enva folds it into Recovery, because it is the same process seen at a different moment.

Recovery answers how well you repaired last night. Battery answers how much of that you have left right now.

Sleep sets the charge

Quality decides where you wake up, not hours. Strong HRV and deep sleep can start you above average on a short night; a fragmented long night might only reach 40 percent.

Training drains it

Workouts cost real resources: glycogen, cardiovascular strain, hormonal output. The harder the session by TRIMP, the deeper the pull.

Existing costs energy too

Baseline metabolism drains the battery even on a rest day, so a rest day never looks artificially perfect.

How it's scored

A bonus for showing up. Never a penalty for a missed day.

Completing an action adds a small bonus to your Enva Health Score, on top of your actual Recovery score.

It rewards the behaviour that produces good recovery, not just the outcome. Skipping an action never subtracts anything. Your Health Score is never worse for a day you didn't tick a box. The list is there to help, not to be one more thing you can fail at.

Where it connects

The input that moves the readout.
