Enva Intelligence

Four services. Four answers. None of them talking.

Every service you pay for holds one piece of the picture and stops at its own edge. Enva Intelligence is the layer that reads across all of them, understands the goal you're training toward, and turns it into an answer you can act on today.

Your apps

Every app has one piece of the puzzle

The sleep app knows your sleep. The training app knows your workouts. Neither shows how one is moving the other.

Your labs

Blood work is disconnected from everything

Your panel never meets your fitness, your nutrition or your sleep, where the numbers would actually mean something.

Your gym

Your workout subscription doesn't know your goal

Peloton, Orangetheory and the rest program for the class in front of you, not the race or target you're working toward.

Your plate

Nutrition never reaches your doctor

What you eat and what your labs say sit in different apps that never compare notes.

One system that pulls it together.

Enva Intelligence weighs every source against each other and against where you're headed, then gives you one specific, actionable answer. The same intelligence runs through the whole app, so it always knows where you are in your fitness journey: on your Health Score, on every workout you finish, on your next session.

What makes it work

Better inputs in. Better outputs out.

Direct device connections

Apple Health flattens your data to a generic "workout." Enva connects to each ecosystem directly and keeps what they actually capture: Peloton output and leaderboard rank, Orangetheory splat points, rower splits and tread pace.
