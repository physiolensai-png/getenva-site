Quick start

Six steps to a useful first day

Do these in order. Each one unlocks something the next step builds on.

  1. 1

    Connect Apple Health

    Tap Allow All when Health asks. Enva reads sleep, heart rate, HRV, workouts and steps from it. The first sync pulls your last 90 days, and older history keeps filling in quietly in the background.

    What you should see: a sync sheet that you can dismiss. Your Health Score appears on Home once there is enough data.

  2. 2

    Read your Health Score

    Tap the score on Home. It combines Readiness, Load and Trajectory, and shows how confident it is. A lower confidence label just means Enva has seen less of your data so far. It rises as the days go on.

    What you should see: three pillar bars and a plain-language reason for each.

  3. 3

    Set one goal

    Open Goals and pick a type: body, event, or individual. Event goals such as Hyrox shape your training phases and your daily fueling advice, so they are the best way to see Enva adapt.

    What you should see: a phase plan and a goal card on Home.

  4. 4

    Log one meal

    Go to Nutrition and log a meal by search, barcode, or Estimate with Enva Intelligence when you only know roughly what you ate. Totals update straight away on Home.

    What you should see: calorie and macro rings that move after you save.

  5. 5

    Do one workout

    Record it on your Apple Watch or phone, or just do your normal session and let it sync. Open it from Training to see heart rate zones, load and recovery.

    What you should see: zone breakdown and a summary for the session.

  6. 6

    Ask Enva Intelligence

    Tap any insight card, or open the chat from Home, and ask something real: "Why is my recovery low today?" or "What should I eat after this workout?" It sees your scores, sleep, training and nutrition.

    What you should see: an answer that cites your own numbers. The daily message limit shows in the chat.

Explore

Find features by what you want to do

Open a section. Each one lists what is there and where to find it.

I want to understand my recovery
  • Sleep and Recovery: stages, trends, and a recovery gauge. No wearable? Log sleep by hand for a lower-confidence score.
  • Recovery actions: a daily checklist on Home. Check things off as you do them.
  • Battery and Stress: energy through the day and a three-signal stress index.

I want to track nutrition
  • Logging: search, barcode, restaurant menus, alcohol, supplements by label scan, and recipes you reuse.
  • Targets: a three-step wizard sets your calories, macros and micronutrients from your body and goal.
  • Trends: TDEE, macro and micronutrient charts. Drag across any chart to read a day.

  • Training tab: every workout grouped by month, with load against recovery.
  • Workout builder: a four-step wizard and 13 curated templates. The AI wizard can build your own.
  • Strength logging: sets, reps and weight, left and right sides, and screenshot import of a plan.

What to test

Where your eyes help most

These are the newest or least-tested parts of this build. If one works, you do not need to say so. If one feels wrong, report it.

  • Apple Watch workouts. Finish one and confirm the summary appears and the workout shows in the Health app.
  • Lab import from Health Records. Only if you have records linked in Apple Health.
  • Oura Ring connection. Only if you own one. Check heart rate, steps and sleep.
  • Strength plan import. Screenshot a plan and check that the numbers match what you sent.
  • Estimate with Enva Intelligence. Compare an estimate against a meal you know.
Feedback

Two ways to report what happened

On an AI answer

Tap the thumbs up or down under any Enva Intelligence response. After a thumbs down you can open a support ticket in one tap and attach screenshots.

Anything else

Take a screenshot in TestFlight (Share, then Send Beta Feedback), or use Report a bug in Settings. Either one includes your build and device automatically.

A good report says what you tapped, what you expected, and what happened instead. One sentence each is plenty.

Known issues

Known rough edges

  • The first sync can take a few minutes on a large Health history. You can dismiss the sheet and keep using the app.
  • Health Score confidence stays low for the first several days. That is expected.
  • Timed strength rows do not yet save seconds.
