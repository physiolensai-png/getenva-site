# See how it all connects.

*For iPhone and Apple Watch*

Enva is a health tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. It brings your training, food, recovery and blood work into one Health Score and one Biological Age, and an AI assistant that answers from all of it.

7 days free when we launch. Then $12.99 a month.

**The score**

## Your age in years. Your day in one number.

Biological Age shows where your habits are taking you over months. The Health Score shows how today is going. Both come from the same sleep, training, nutrition and lab data, and both explain what moved them.

- Biological Age estimate that improves as you add labs
- Daily Health Score that works with a watch alone
- Scored on your phone, not on a server

**What goes in**

## Log it once. It counts everywhere.

Scan, search or describe a meal and the day's calories, macros and micronutrients update as you go. Strength sets, Orangetheory, Peloton and Watch workouts land in one history, with no double counting.

- Food, supplements and micronutrients
- Strength sets, with your progress tracked over time
- Orangetheory classes, including your treadmill and rower data
- Peloton rides and Apple Watch workouts
- Body scans, including hydrostatic testing and DEXA

**Blood work**

## Your labs, next to your training.

Bring in results from Apple Health Records, or upload a PDF or a photo of a lab report. Anything Enva cannot match is listed by name, never guessed.

- Cholesterol, glucose, hormones and more
- Blood pressure, logged and tracked over time
- Trends once you have two results
- Delete any result at any time

**Enva Intelligence**

## An assistant that has read all of it.

Ask in plain language. Enva reads your workouts, food, sleep and labs together, and answers from your own numbers.

You ask what to focus on in training. Enva reads 18 sessions, 7 nights of sleep, your HRV baseline, your food log and your latest blood panel, then gives one answer.

### Build your workout plan.

Around your goal, your schedule and the classes you already love. Ask for a Hyrox block, a strength cycle or a lighter week.

Or tell Enva what equipment you have, from a full gym to a single pair of dumbbells, and it builds the session around exactly that.

### Find your next meal.

High protein, low fat, and sized to the calories you have left today. Log it with one tap.

Add your own recipes, or ask for new ones based on what you have on hand. Turn any recipe into a shopping list and untick what you already own. Eating out? Describe the meal and Enva estimates it for you.

### Connect everything.

One answer from your watch, your food log and your lab results, together.

### Your data stays yours.

- Scored on your device
- Never used to train models
- Delete everything anytime

**Quick answers**

## Before you join.

The short version. [More answers in Support](https://getenva.ai/support).

### How much does Enva cost?

Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial, then it is $12.99 a month or $89.99 a year (about $7.50 a month). Both plans include every feature, and you can cancel anytime in your Apple settings. One trial per customer.

### Which devices and apps does Enva work with?

Enva is built for iPhone and Apple Watch and reads your data from Apple Health. At launch it also connects to Oura Ring, Peloton and Orangetheory, with Garmin, WHOOP and Fitbit coming soon. You do not need an Apple Watch to use Enva, but starting a workout from your wrist does.

### Is there an Android version of Enva?

No. Enva is for iPhone and Apple Watch only. Join the waitlist and we will email you when it launches.

### Is Enva medical advice?

No. Enva is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. A result outside its usual range is a reason to talk to a clinician, not a conclusion.

### Where does my health data go?

Your Health Score and Biological Age are worked out on your phone. Enva Intelligence is off until you turn it on, it receives summaries rather than raw sensor data, and your data is never used to train models. Read the [privacy policy](https://getenva.ai/privacy#enva-intelligence).
