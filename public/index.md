**What goes in**

## Log it once. It counts everywhere.

Scan, search or describe a meal and the day's calories, macros and micronutrients update as you go. Strength sets, Orangetheory, Peloton and Watch workouts land in one history, with no double counting.

- Food, supplements and micronutrients
- Strength sets, with your progress tracked over time
- Orangetheory classes, including your treadmill and rower data
- Peloton rides and Apple Watch workouts
- Body scans, including hydrostatic testing and DEXA

**Blood work**

## Your labs, next to your training.

Bring in results from Apple Health Records, or upload a PDF or a photo of a lab report. Anything Enva cannot match is listed by name, never guessed.

- Cholesterol, glucose, hormones and more
- Blood pressure, logged and tracked over time
- Trends once you have two results
- Delete any result at any time

**Enva Intelligence**

## An assistant that has read all of it.

Ask in plain language. Enva reads your workouts, food, sleep and labs together, and answers from your own numbers.

You ask what to focus on in training. Enva reads 18 sessions, 7 nights of sleep, your HRV baseline, your food log and your latest blood panel, then gives one answer.

### Build your workout plan.

Around your goal, your schedule and the classes you already love. Ask for a Hyrox block, a strength cycle or a lighter week.

Or tell Enva what equipment you have, from a full gym to a single pair of dumbbells, and it builds the session around exactly that.

### Find your next meal.

High protein, low fat, and sized to the calories you have left today. Log it with one tap.

Add your own recipes, or ask for new ones based on what you have on hand. Turn any recipe into a shopping list and untick what you already own. Eating out? Describe the meal and Enva estimates it for you.

### Connect everything.

One answer from your watch, your food log and your lab results, together.

### Your data stays yours.

- Scored on your device
- Never used to train models
- Delete everything anytime
