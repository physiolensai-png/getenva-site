What goes into it

Three pillars, each measured against your own baseline.

Not just recovered. Not just active. Trained well, recovering well, and heading the right way, weighted into one number and scored against you, not a population chart.

Readiness · 40%

How recovered you are

A 7-day rolling average of composite recovery, weighted by sleep quality and prior-day training load.

Load Quality · 35%

Whether this week's volume fits

How well your weekly training matches your 42-day Chronic Training Load. Rewards the 70 to 130% band and training variety.

Trajectory · 25%

The direction you're heading

The 6-week trend across VO₂ Max, morning HRV and training-load direction. A build or a taper is read in context, not as a decline.

Completing your daily Recovery Actions adds a small bonus on top. Skipping them never subtracts, so there is only upside to trying.

Making sense of your score

A lower score after a hard week is not a warning.

A dip after a hard training block is the pillar breakdown doing its job. It tells you which pillar moved, and whether that was expected.

First

See which pillar is down

Load Quality falling after a big week is expected. Readiness low when you have not trained hard is worth a closer look.

Then

Read it in context

Enva knows if you are in a build, a taper or a race week, and reads the trend against that instead of flagging a decline.

Watch for

Readiness recovering

When Readiness climbs back while Load Quality stays high, your body is adapting. That is the signal you want.

Every score shows its work

The reasoning, and the confidence.

Tap the score and Enva Intelligence breaks down what is dragging it down, what is holding it up, and the single biggest lever. Next to it: exactly how much of your data the score is standing on.

How it's calculated

The formula, in full.

Readiness40% of the score

Average recovery score across 7 days, weighted by sleep quality and effort delta.

Load Quality35% of the score

Weekly TRIMPexp against your 42-day Chronic Training Load.

Trajectory25% of the score

Linear trend in VO₂ Max, HRV and CTL over 6 weeks. VO₂ Max is normalised per measurement method; build, taper and illness windows are read in context.

The score updates each time you open Insights, and needs at least three nights of recent sleep data to show.
