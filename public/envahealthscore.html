What goes into it

Three pillars, weighted. Your data, not a population chart.

The Health Score answers the question no single metric can: how well are you actually doing across all dimensions this week? Not just recovered, not just active. Trained well, recovering well, trending in the right direction. It is built from three components, each measured against your own baseline.

40%

Readiness

How recovered you are today, based on sleep quality, HRV, and resting heart rate trend.

35%

Training Load

Your fitness-to-fatigue ratio, measured using TRIMP across your recent training sessions.

25%

Trajectory

The direction your health is heading, calculated from your 30 to 60-day trend.

Completing your daily Recovery Actions adds a small bonus on top of this score. Skipping them never subtracts. There is only upside to trying.

Making sense of your score

A lower score after a hard week is not a warning.

A lower score after a hard training week isn't a health warning. The pillar breakdown tells you exactly why, and what to do next.

  1. Step 01

    See which pillar is down

    Tap the score to open the breakdown. If Training Load is pulling it down after a big week, that is expected. If Readiness is low and you have not trained hard, that is worth paying attention to.

  2. Step 02

    Ask the AI for context

    AI Chat has your full training history. Ask what your score means this week and it will factor in how hard you have been working.

  3. Step 03

    Watch Readiness recover

    When Readiness bounces back over the next few days while Training Load stays elevated, your body is adapting. That is the signal you are looking for.
