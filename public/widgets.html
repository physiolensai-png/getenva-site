On your Lock Screen

Small glances that stay put.

Circle and rectangle widgets keep one number in view, like resting heart rate, HRV or protein, before you unlock your phone.

Add one

Four taps to a widget.

  1. Press and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen until the icons wiggle. For a Lock Screen widget, press and hold the Lock Screen instead and tap Customize.
  2. Tap the plus in the top corner, then search for Enva.
  3. Pick a widget, swipe to the size you want, and tap Add Widget.
  4. Press and hold the widget, then tap Edit Widget to change what it shows. Only the ones tagged Configurable can be changed.

Widgets show the data Enva last synced. If one looks empty, open Enva once and give it a moment.
