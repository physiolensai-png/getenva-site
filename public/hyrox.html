Goal Setting

What feeds your goal

Every signal, one question.

Once the goal is set, Enva stops reporting numbers and starts answering one thing: what is holding this back right now.

Zone distribution
Whether your easy work is genuinely easy. Hyrox punishes an aerobic base built entirely at threshold — most people training for it live in the wrong zone.
Recovery
HRV, resting heart rate, and post-effort drop — read against your own baseline, not a population chart.
Sleep
Duration, consistency, and the deep and REM split. The part that decides whether last week's training became fitness or just fatigue.
Nutrition
Calories against real expenditure, and whether protein is supporting the strength side while you hold a deficit.
Load balance
Running against stations against everything else. Progressive overload only works when the progression is visible across both.
Labs
Ferritin, vitamin D, thyroid, inflammatory markers. When the training is right and the times are not moving, this is usually where the answer sits.
