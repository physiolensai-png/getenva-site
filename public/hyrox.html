Watch App

Every split, right on your wrist.

Hyrox is eight runs and eight stations, and the race is won or lost by how well you run each segment after the last one. The Enva watch app runs the full race alongside you — each segment gets a target pace calibrated to your training, not a generic bracket.

Go out too hot on run one and you see it immediately. Nail your SkiErg split and move into the next run knowing exactly where you are against the target and what you have left.

  • Per-segment pacing based on your real fitness data
  • HR zone feedback in context of the station effort
  • Station preview so you know what is coming next
  • Race mode and training mode — same interface, different thresholds

On-Demand Workouts

Train what you have, where you have it.

Most Hyrox training advice assumes you have a competition gym every day. Enva starts from what you actually have — the equipment available right now, the gap you need to close, and how far out the race is.

Pick your equipment and your focus. Enva generates a session that maps to the real station sequence and the compromised running that follows every one of them. Not a generic circuit — work that targets the specific minutes slipping away.

  • Builds from any gym, garage, hotel, or hotel gym
  • Focus on stations, compromised runs, or aerobic base
  • Sessions that simulate the race load, not just the movements
  • Adapts to where you are in the training block
Goal Setting

One finish time. Everything reads through it.

Enter your target time and the date. From that point every metric Enva has — zone distribution, HRV trend, nutrition balance — stops being a number and becomes an answer to one question: is this moving you closer or further away.

Enva is not telling you to sleep more. It is telling you that the 3:40 gap to your target traces specifically to zone 2 volume, and that closing it means three additional 45-minute sessions before the taper.

  • Works backwards from your target to build weekly priorities
  • Names the single biggest limiter, not a list of ten things
  • Adjusts the projection as your data changes week to week
  • Supports Solo, Doubles, Mixed Doubles, and Pro divisions

What feeds your goal

Every signal, pointed at the same finish line.

Once you have a goal, Enva stops reporting numbers and starts answering one question: what is holding this back right now.

Zone distribution
Whether your easy work is genuinely easy. Hyrox punishes an aerobic base built entirely at threshold, and most people training for it are living in the wrong zone.
Recovery
HRV, resting heart rate, and how fast your heart drops after effort — read against your own baseline, not a population chart.
Sleep
Duration, consistency, and the deep and REM split. The part that determines whether last week's training turned into fitness or just fatigue.
Nutrition
Calories against your real expenditure, and whether protein is actually supporting the strength side while you hold a deficit.
Load balance
Running against stations against everything else. Progressive overload only works when the progression is visible across both.
Labs
Ferritin, vitamin D, thyroid, inflammatory markers. When the training is right and the times still are not moving, this is usually where the answer sits.
