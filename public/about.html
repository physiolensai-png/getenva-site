Our story

One person got tired of being
his own data scientist.

Enva exists because the tools we use to track our health don't talk to each other, and piecing it together by hand every morning is exhausting.

The problem

Health is holistic. The tools are not.

Your training, your sleep, your nutrition and your blood work each live in a different app. Even a doctor's visit ends with a PDF that connects to nothing: no link to last night's HRV, this week's training load, or how you've been recovering.

Your bike doesn't know about the HRV dip. Your nutrition app doesn't know you just finished a hard block. And none of them have seen your labs.

The data existed. The insight didn't. No single tool could tell me how these things were actually affecting each other.

How it started

Screenshots, every morning.

Marcus found himself taking screenshots of half a dozen apps every day, building his own templates, and feeding all of it into a chatbot just to force the numbers to talk to each other.

The answers were genuinely useful. The process was miserable. There had to be a better way to take all of those separate inputs and turn them into one coherent read, without doing the assembly by hand.

That is exactly what Enva became.

What changed

The assembly happens on its own now.

Enva connects directly to each source, keeps the detail the others flatten, and reads it all together. It answers four questions in one system, from what happened last night to what your data says about how you're aging: your Sleep Score, your Recovery, your Enva Health Score for the week, and your Biological Age over time.

The intelligence runs through all of it. It knows your goals, your recent training zones and your lab values, and it connects them, the way you used to try to do by pasting screenshots into a chat window.

The promise

You own your data.

Health data is too often mined, aggregated and sold. Enva rejects that model. Summaries and scores are computed on your device. When deeper analysis has to leave your phone, it goes to an enterprise-secure model, never used to train anything, never sold. You can export everything, and delete everything.

From the founder

Cheers,

I built Enva because I was the person taking screenshots every morning, pasting data into AI tools just to understand my own body. The frustration was never that the data didn't exist. It was that no one had built the thing that made it make sense together.

Enva is that thing. It's for the person who knows what ApoB means and wants it in the same view as their VO₂ Max trend. It's for the Orangetheory member who wants more than splat points. And it's for anyone who has ever stood at the start of a fitness journey wishing someone would just show them where to begin.

Whatever brought you here, I built this for you.

Marcus
Founder, Enva
