About Enva

I was tired of being my own data scientist.

Every morning I stitched my training, sleep, food and blood work together by hand. Enva is the tool I built so nobody has to. It is a health app for iPhone and Apple Watch, made by Olympus Hills Ventures.

Why I built it

The data existed. The insight didn't.

Every morning I took screenshots. Output from the bike. The workout from the studio. HRV from Apple Health. The strength plan was a Reddit post, and the weights I lifted were typed into a note. Then I pasted all of it into an AI chat to force the numbers to talk to each other.

The answers were useful. The process was miserable, and no single tool held all of it. Enva does that assembly for me now, and for you. See how it works.

What it stands on

Three promises.

1

Your data stays yours.

Scores are computed on your phone. Enva never sells your data or uses it to train AI models. If you turn on Enva Intelligence, it sends computed summaries, not raw sensor data, to a third-party AI provider to answer your questions. You can wipe everything from your device at any time. See exactly what is sent.

2

It tells you how sure it is.

A score built on less data shows lower confidence instead of guessing, so you know how much to trust it. See how the scores work.

3

It is not medical advice.

Enva is a wellness app, not a medical device. A result outside its usual range is a reason to talk to a clinician, not a conclusion.
