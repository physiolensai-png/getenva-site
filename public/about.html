Health is holistic.
Our tools are not.

Health, wellness, and fitness data is scattered across half a dozen different apps and wearables. Even when you visit a doctor, blood work arrives as an isolated report — a PDF with no connection to your sleep quality, training load, or daily recovery patterns.

Your Peloton doesn't know about last night's HRV dip. Your nutrition app doesn't know you just finished a hard block of training. And none of them have seen your labs.

"The data existed. The insight didn't. No single tool could tell me how these factors actually influenced each other."

The Origin Story

Enva was born out of that frustration. Marcus found himself taking screenshots of multiple apps every day, just to piece together a complete view of how his body was performing. He started building custom templates and feeding those screenshots into generative AI to force disparate data points to talk to each other.

The insights were genuinely useful. The process was exhausting. There had to be a better way to take all of these distinct inputs and seamlessly turn them into a single, cohesive output.

That's exactly how Enva was created.

What We Built

The entire journey of building Enva was driven by a single goal: transforming all of your health inputs into actionable analysis. No manual templates. No screenshots. No stitching together data yourself.

What is all of this adding up to?

The AI coaching layer goes beyond generic advice. It knows your goals, your recent training zones, your lab values — and it connects them. "Based on your fat loss goals, your past two weeks were heavy on Zone 1 and Zone 4/5. You need more Zone 2 this week — it's key to fat oxidation." That's the difference between a dashboard and a coach.

Our Core Promise:
You Own Your Data

In today's tech landscape, personal health data is too often treated as a commodity — mined, aggregated, and sold to the highest bidder. Enva fundamentally rejects that model.

All calculations and summaries run on-device. When deeper AI analysis leaves your phone, it goes to an enterprise-secure LLM environment. Your data is never used to train models. It is never sold. It never becomes someone else's product.

By prioritizing on-device processing and strict privacy protocols, we ensure you get the immense benefits of AI analysis without ever sacrificing your personal privacy.
